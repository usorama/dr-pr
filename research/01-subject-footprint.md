# Baseline audit — public footprint of Dr. Avinash Paturi / Dr. Pathuri Avinash (as of 12 Nov 2026)

**Executive summary**

1. He is findable, but only as a **directory entry**: Apollo Hospitals, Apollo 24|7, Practo, Vaidam, Citizens Hospitals, Eka Care and DocIndia. Nothing found that he owns or controls (no personal site, no ORCID, no author profile), and no general name search surfaced LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram, X, news or Telugu coverage.
2. The listings **contradict each other** on almost every NAP field: name order/spelling (3 variants), hospital (5 locations incl. two stale Rajahmundry entries), experience (4 / 6 / 6+ / 10 / 14 years), degree-awarding university, registration year, procedure numbers and specialty label.
3. His verifiable scholarly record is thin but real: **one first-author case report (JCDR 2018, as "Avinash Pathuri")** and a **listing as a PROMINENT trial investigator in NEJM 2022** (PubMed investigator field, "Pathuri, Avinash"). Vaidam overstates this as "co-authored" plus an "award" that does not appear to exist — a credibility risk.
4. Name collisions are real for the spelling **"Avinash Paturi"** (a St. George's University author with an ORCID, a University of Hyderabad kidney researcher "Paturi A/ASV", a 2021 IJSR case report of unknown ownership). The spelling "Pathuri Avinash" is almost collision-free and already owns page 1.
5. **Audit is incomplete**: the shared web-search budget ran out after 10 queries. Google Business Profile/reviews, Justdial, Lybrate, Sehat, Credihealth, Bajaj Finserv Health, LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, news (English/Telugu), CSI/TCT abstracts and the medical-council register were **not audited** — they are marked "not audited", not "absent".

## Risks & things to avoid

- **Overstated research claims.** "Co-authored a NEJM paper" is checkable in 30 seconds and is not accurate; he is a listed trial investigator. Correct it before any PR push draws scrutiny.
- **Unverifiable awards and unaudited procedure numbers.** Two different sets of volumes are already public. Publish only figures he can back from cath-lab logs, with a date.
- **Experience inflation by aggregators.** "14 years" next to "6+ years" looks careless or misleading; standardise on "6+ years as cardiologist (DM 2020); in medicine since 2012".
- **Renaming to "Paturi" without a bridge** would orphan his existing search results and merge him with namesakes in scholarly indexes.
- **Wrong-city listings** send patients to Rajahmundry and dilute local search signals for Hyderabad.
- **Do not solicit or script reviews** to pad weak platforms; fix facts first. (Regulatory detail belongs to the ethics/regulation research file.)
- **Do not treat "not audited" rows as "absent".** Several conclusions here rest on four searches.
