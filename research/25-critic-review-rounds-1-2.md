# 25: Critic's review of research files 01–19 (Rounds 1–3)

**Date:** 12 November 2026. **Scope:** files 00 (brief and all four addenda), 01–19 including 11b. Files 20–24 were still being written and are not reviewed here.
**Method:** I read each file's executive summary, "So what", "Risks" and "Open questions" in full and skimmed its Findings headings. I read Findings in detail only where I needed to check a contradiction (05 §6, 11 §3, 13 §3–4, 16 §3.4–5, 17 §0–5 and §8, 19 sections A–C summary). I used four web lookups to settle contradictions: two fetches (YouTube and vidIQ for @Dr.MovvaSrinivas) and two searches (Lp(a)HORIZON; *MIOT v. Palaniappan*). Results are given in place, with sources at the end.

**Verdict in one paragraph.** The regulatory work (02, 11, 11b) is careful and agrees with itself, and the "teach, don't sell" lane is well founded. The plan is not yet buildable, for four reasons:
- **Time and labour.** Each file sizes its own workstream as if it were the only one. Added up, the doctor would need about 15–20 hours a week and the friend about 15–25, which is not realistic.
- **Apollo dependence.** Much of the most detailed execution depends on Apollo, which the sponsor says he is leaving: the ethics committee, NBEMS teaching status, the hospital nurse lead, the Apollo PR flow, the Apollo YouTube channel and the Citizens training centre.
- **Unverified facts about him.** Several load-bearing facts are unconfirmed: whether he holds Telangana registration, what his own practice actually is, what his procedure numbers are, and whether the friend has the time.
- **Conflicting channel advice.** The files disagree on the primary format and channel. The synthesis must choose; it cannot average them.

## Part 7: Draft skeleton of Principles, Objectives and Rules (as the evidence stands)

### Principles (11)
- **P1. Teach, don't sell.** Reputation is a by-product of public education, scholarship, teaching and service. Every item must be worth publishing even if no patient ever came because of it. *[02 Rule 1; 06; 11; Addendum 1]*
- **P2. Build on what he owns.** Every asset (name, domain, accounts, lists, papers, programmes, data) must travel with him. Hospital platforms are temporary bridges, never foundations. *[17 §4; 10 Risks; Addendum 2; C3]*
- **P3. Numbers before opinions.** Every claim carries its source and its size. Risk is stated as "x in 100", never as fear. *[14 §4; 19 Principles 2 and 5; 02 Red (fear)]*
- **P4. Information plus a route to action.** Education is attached to measurement, a route to treatment, human follow-up and family or peer units, or it is not called a programme. *[19 Exec 1; 15 Exec 3]*
- **P5. Privacy is absolute.** No identifiable patient appears anywhere public, with or without consent. Real cases go only to journals and peer meetings under protocol. *[02 cl. 6.2; 11 §2; 19 Principle 8]*
- **P6. He approves everything.** The friend and AI draft; he signs. The log proves it. *[02 Rule 11; 11; 06]*
- **P7. Sustainable pace over volume.** Output stays at a level he can personally read. Use a fixed slot, a buffer and "never miss twice". *[06 §7; 11 Risks (scale); 18 Risks (volume); C10]*
- **P8. Telugu is first-class and verified.** Every Telugu item gets a native medical reviewer and a listener test. *[14 Risks; 13; 05]*
- **P9. Free, open and sponsor-free.** No brand, product or company in any public or trainee teaching. Disclose everything. *[02 Rule 6 and Rule 13; 16 Rules 10–12; 19 Principle 7]*
- **P10. Argue against your own interest when the evidence does, and credit others by name.** Use "when you do not need a stent" with its pair, decision aids, and named sources. *[14 §5E; 19 Principles 4 and 6]*
- **P11. Publish results honestly, including nulls, and correct errors in public.** *[19 Principles 3 and 9; 09 Exec 4]*
