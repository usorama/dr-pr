# Action plan: 13 November 2026 to 2029

**Who this plan is for.** This plan is for Dr. Avinash Pathuri and his friend. The spelling of his name is still to be confirmed (see `synthesis.md` §2). Read this plan together with two other files. `synthesis.md` explains the reasons for each step. `questions-and-open-items.md` lists the questions that only he or his friend can answer.

## How to use this plan

This section explains the four stages, the weekly time, the people, the costs and the sources.

**The four stages.** Day 1 is Friday 13 November 2026. The plan has four stages:

- Stage 1 is days 1 to 30. It runs from 13 November 2026 to 12 December 2026.
- Stage 2 is days 31 to 90. It runs from 13 December 2026 to 10 February 2027.
- Stage 3 is months 4 to 12. It runs from 11 February 2027 to 12 November 2027.
- Stage 4 is years 2 and 3. It runs from 13 November 2027 to November 2029.

The action numbers still begin with 0, 1, 2 and 3 for Stages 1 to 4. For example, action 0.6 belongs to Stage 1, and action 2.2a belongs to Stage 3.

**Weekly time.** This plan assumes that he gives 4 hours a week and his friend gives 6 hours a week. He must confirm these numbers. In Stage 1, he needs about 4 to 6 hours a week for paperwork that is done only once [21]. The last table shows the plan for 2 hours and for 6 hours a week.

**People.** The "Who" column uses these words:

- "He" means Dr. Avinash Pathuri.
- "His friend" means the friend who helps him.
- "Clinic manager" means his clinic manager or a senior member of his staff.
- "Clinic nurse" means the nurse or coordinator in his clinic.
- "Lawyer" means a health lawyer.
- "Accountant" means a chartered accountant.
- "Telugu reviewer" means the person who checks all Telugu text.
- "Second doctor" means a second doctor who checks his work before it is published.

**Money.** All costs are in Indian rupees (Rs). One lakh is 100,000. One crore is 100 lakh, that is, 10 million. In the cost column, "estimate" marks an estimate. "Published fee" marks a fee that the organisation publishes. "Assumption" marks a figure that this plan assumes.

**Sources.** The "Source" column gives the number of a research file in the folder `research/`. A second part points to a section or row in that file. For example, "21 A6" means file 21, row A6. Numbers in square brackets, such as [21], also point to these files.

**Labels for legal points.** This plan uses three labels for legal points:

- **The rule says**: the words are in the rule itself.
- **Our reading**: the rule does not say it in these words. This is how we read it. A lawyer should confirm.
- **Not decided**: the rule does not answer the question, and no authority has answered it.

**The condition before public teaching starts.** He publishes nothing that carries his name and his registration line until four conditions are met. The registration line is the line that gives his name, degrees, registration number and medical council. The four conditions are listed at the start of Stage 2. The earliest possible start of public teaching is the week that begins on Monday 18 January 2027.
