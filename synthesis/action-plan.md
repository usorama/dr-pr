## The 5–10 year horizon

By 2031–2036 the realistic aspiration (to be confirmed by his answer to question I-1) has six parts:

- an **independent heart clinic with a measured prevention layer**;
- a **teaching series that outlives his weekly involvement**, run by an editorial group and a society;
- an **open Telugu library** of patient explanations, decision aids and posters used by other clinics and the state system;
- **a body of peer-reviewed audits** that makes FESC, FACC and later FCSI a matter of record;
- a **policy role** in Telangana's cardiac-arrest, STEMI or NCD (non-communicable disease) programmes, earned through counted results (the Himachal programme moved thrombolysis from 8 of 29 to 167 of 255 patients with weekly virtual teaching [31 1.14]);
- possibly an **academic appointment** (adjunct now; "Professor of Practice" from about 2031 under the 15-year rule) [29].

A charitable entity, a book and a named lecture are by-products of that record, never targets in themselves.
