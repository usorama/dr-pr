Rules for doctors in India · 6 October 2026

What a doctor may and may not say in public

Allowed

  • Teach people about health, in his own name
  • Publish in medical journals and speak at medical conferences
  • Use invented patient examples, clearly marked as examples
  • Show a signboard, his fees and the facts about his clinic
  • Announce a fact, such as a new clinic address
  • Ask patients in private how his care can improve

Not clear. Ask a lawyer first.

  • A friend edits his posts, and AI writes the first draft
  • Earning advertising money from YouTube
  • Telling the public his results, after a journal has published them
  • Offering free health tests in a group programme
  • Charging the public for a course

Not allowed

  • Publishing patient stories or patient praise
  • Asking patients for public reviews, or sharing their praise
  • Words such as “best” or “top”, and advertising success rates
  • Taking sponsors or recommending products
  • Using his teaching to bring patients to his clinic

The rules come from the National Medical Commission. They apply together with the 2002 regulations for doctors. Owning a clinic does not make them less strict.

Who may say what

Other people may speak freely about him. The rules limit only what he does.

Other people may do this, if it is their own choice

  • A patient may write a review, praise him or criticise him.
  • A young doctor he taught may thank him or write about his teaching.
  • A medical society may invite him to speak or give him an award.
  • A journalist may report on his published work.

It breaks the rules if he does any of these

  • He asks them to say it.
  • He writes the words for them.
  • He gives them something in return.
  • He reposts it, quotes it or links to it.

What he himself may do

Allowed

  • Teach the public and write about new research
  • Show his results to other doctors, without patient names
  • Ask patients for private feedback
  • Accept an award, and thank a young doctor in public

Ask a lawyer first

  • Tell the public his results, after a journal publishes them
  • Reply to a kind review, or “like” it
  • List his awards on his own website

Not allowed

  • Ask for a public review, or show praise on his pages
  • Advertise success rates or patient numbers

The safe order for sharing his results

1. The patient agrees2. An ethics committee approves3. A journal or conference publishes it4. He may mention it in public teaching

If a patient asks where to write a review: he does not ask for one, does not suggest any words, gives nothing in return, and does not repost it. The rules are not clear about asking for public reviews, so this plan does not allow it.

Four ways of teaching that no other Telugu heart doctor uses

“After the stent”

A course in 10 numbered parts for patients who have had a stent.

“The first 60 minutes”

He trains people, with their own hands, in what to do when a heart stops.

“Know your numbers”

One Telugu card every week. Each card shows its sources and his registration number.

Case discussions for doctors

Every two weeks, doctors meet online and discuss one real heart case, with the patient’s details removed. Any registered doctor may join, free of charge. The public cannot join.

He will not try to post short videos every day. Another heart doctor already does that well.

Most people with heart risk do not have it under control

12.6%

Of 100 adults with high blood pressure, about 13 have it under control.

27%

Of 100 treated heart patients, 27 reach the target for LDL cholesterol (the harmful kind).

70%

Of 100 patients with a stent, 70 still take their statin (a cholesterol medicine) after six months.

Each block of dots is 100 people. Red dots are patients who stopped the medicine. The statin figure comes from one study.

The main programmes

He starts with his own patients, then adds one programme at a time

1

For his patients, for life

Starts first, in his own clinic
  • A one-page summary of the patient’s heart condition
  • An explanation in simple Telugu, checked and signed by him
  • Phone calls on day 3, 30, 90, 180 and 365
  • His own record of how patients do over time
2

For young doctors

Starts in March 2027
  • A 45-minute case discussion every two weeks, for doctors only
  • A public page of teaching cases, using images that are free to share
  • About 440 heart-specialist trainees study in the Telugu states
3

For the public: Heart School

Starts in the second year
  • A group of 50 people, for 12 weeks
  • Health numbers are measured at the start, then after 3, 6 and 12 months
  • A family member joins each person
  • People at risk are sent to their own doctor

“The first 60 minutes”

Once a month
  • People practise CPR with their hands. CPR means pressing the chest when a heart stops.
  • 300 to 500 people trained in the first year
  • The host organisation reports the numbers, not he

A new programme starts only when the earlier one runs well. He must decide one thing: should Heart School start before the programme for young doctors?

How he uses AI

Patient information never mixes with public teaching

1 in 4

The best AI tools get about 1 Telugu word in 4 wrong. So a person must check all Telugu.

He will not use:

an AI copy of his facean AI copy of his voicea public chatbot

Any doctor can buy AI tools. His advantage is careful checking. He will publish his rules for using AI. He will also show other doctors how he checks an explanation that AI wrote, including mistakes the AI made.
